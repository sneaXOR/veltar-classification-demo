# Category 5 Part 1 — Telecommunications

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

Note 1: Control status of components, test and "production" equipment and "software" for telecommunications equipment is determined in Category 5, Part 1.
Note 2: "Digital computers" essential for operation of telecommunications equipment are regarded as specially designed components (standard models customarily supplied by manufacturer).

## 5A1 — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 5A001 — Telecommunications systems, equipment, components and accessories

a. Any telecommunications equipment: 1. specially designed to withstand nuclear EMP; 2. specially hardened for gamma/neutron/ion radiation; 3. designed for operation below 218K (-55°C); or 4. above 397K (124°C).
   Notes: a.3. and a.4. control only electronic equipment. a.2.-a.4. do not control equipment for satellites.

b. Telecommunication systems/equipment:
   1. Underwater untethered communications: acoustic carrier outside 20-60 kHz; electromagnetic <30 kHz; electronic beam steering; or lasers/LEDs 400-700nm in LAN
   2. Radio equipment 1.5-87.5 MHz: auto frequency/data rate prediction AND linear power amp >1kW (1.5-30 MHz) or >250W (30-87.5 MHz) over ≥1 octave with harmonics <-80 dB
   3. "Spread spectrum" radio (including "frequency hopping"): user programmable spreading codes OR total bandwidth ≥100x information channel bandwidth and >50 kHz. Note: does not control ≤1W output.
   4. Ultra-wideband radio: user programmable codes AND bandwidth >500 MHz or fractional bandwidth ≥20%
   5. Digitally controlled radio receivers: >1000 channels, switching time <1ms, auto searching/scanning, signal/transmitter identification
   6. Voice coding <700 bit/s

c. Optical fibres >500m withstanding 'proof test' tensile stress ≥2x10^9 N/m2

d. 'Electronically steerable phased array antennae': 1. >31.8-57 GHz with ERP ≥+20 dBm; 2. >57-66 GHz with ERP ≥+24 dBm; 3. >66-90 GHz with ERP ≥+20 dBm; 4. >90 GHz
   Note: does not control antennae for civil cellular/WLAN, IEEE 802.15/wireless HDMI, or commercial satellite earth stations.

e. Radio direction finding (>30 MHz, instantaneous bandwidth ≥10 MHz, LOB for ≤1ms signals)

f. Mobile telecommunications interception/jamming and monitoring:
   1. Interception for voice/data extraction over air interface
   2. Interception for client device/subscriber identifiers (IMSI, TIMSI, IMEI)
   3. Jamming equipment simulating RAN, exploiting mobile protocol characteristics
   4. RF monitoring for detecting items in 5A001.f.1-3

g. Passive Coherent Location (PCL) systems detecting/tracking objects via ambient RF reflections

h. Counter-IED equipment: 1. RF transmitters for premature activation/prevention; 2. Equipment enabling radio comms in same frequencies as co-located counter-IED equipment

j. IP network surveillance systems: performing application layer analysis + metadata extraction + indexing on carrier-class IP networks, AND executing "hard selector" searches + relational network mapping

### 5A101 — Telemetry and telecontrol equipment for 'missiles'

## 5B1 — Test, Inspection and Production Equipment
5B001: Equipment for "development"/"production" of 5A001, including "laser" equipment (>1750nm wavelength, analogue >2.5 GHz), QAM >1024

## 5C1 — None

## 5D1 — Software
5D001: For "development"/"production"/"use" of 5A001/5B001, specific telecom "software" for 5A001/5B001 functions, monitoring/analysis software for law enforcement with "hard selector" searches via 'handover interface'
5D101: For "use" of 5A101

## 5E1 — Technology
5E001: For "development"/"production"/"use" of 5A001/5B001/5D001, including satellite telecom, laser communication through exoatmosphere/sub-surface, digital cellular base station, "spread spectrum"
Also: "technology" for "MMIC" amplifiers for telecommunications (same thresholds as 3A001.b.2.)
5E101: For 5A101
