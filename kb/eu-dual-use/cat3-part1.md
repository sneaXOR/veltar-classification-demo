# Category 3 (Part 1) — Electronics: Integrated Circuits and Microwave Items (3A001.a-b)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

Note 1: Control status of equipment/components described in 3A001 or 3A002 (other than 3A001.a.3.-a.10., a.12.-a.14.) which are specially designed for or have same functional characteristics as other equipment is determined by control status of that other equipment.
Note 2: Control status of ICs in 3A001.a.3.-a.9., a.12.-a.14. which are unalterably programmed for specific function is determined by control status of other equipment.

## 3A — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 3A001 — Electronic items

#### 3A001.a — General purpose integrated circuits

1. Radiation hardened ICs: (a) total dose ≥5x10^3 Gy(Si); (b) dose rate upset ≥5x10^6 Gy(Si)/s; or (c) neutron fluence ≥5x10^13 n/cm2
   Note: MIS not controlled for 3A001.a.1.c.

2. Compound semiconductor microprocessors/microcomputers/microcontrollers, storage ICs, ADCs with analogue-to-digital + storage/processing, DACs, electro-optical/"optical ICs" for signal processing, FPLDs, custom ICs (unknown function), FFT processors, SRAMs, 'non-volatile memories': rated for operation at >398K (125°C), <218K (-55°C), or full range 218K-398K.
   Note: Does not control ICs for civil automobiles or railway.

3. Compound semiconductor microprocessor/microcomputer/microcontroller microcircuits operating at clock frequency >40 MHz.

5. ADC and DAC ICs:
   a. ADCs: 8-bit ≥1.3 GSPS; 10-bit ≥600 MSPS; 12-bit ≥400 MSPS; 14-bit ≥250 MSPS; 16-bit ≥65 MSPS
   b. DACs: 10-bit ≥3500 MSPS 'adjusted update rate'; 12-bit with specific SFDR or settling time or ≥3500 MSPS 'adjusted update rate'

6. Electro-optical and "optical ICs" for signal processing (with internal laser diode, light detecting element, and optical waveguides)

7. FPLDs: (a) >700 single-ended digital I/Os; or (b) 'aggregate one-way peak serial transceiver data rate' ≥500 Gb/s

9. Neural network integrated circuits

10. Custom ICs (unknown function/equipment): >1500 terminals, "basic gate propagation delay time" <0.02ns, or operating frequency >3 GHz

11. Compound semiconductor digital ICs: >3000 equivalent gates or toggle frequency >1.2 GHz

12. FFT processors: rated execution time <(N log2 N)/20480 ms

13. DDS ICs: DAC clock ≥3.5 GHz with ≥10-bit <12-bit resolution; or DAC clock ≥1.25 GHz with ≥12-bit resolution

14. ICs performing ADC + storage/processing of digitised data: 8-bit ≥1.3 GSPS; 10-bit ≥1.0 GSPS; 12-bit ≥1.0 GSPS; 14-bit ≥400 MSPS; 16-bit ≥180 MSPS

#### 3A001.b — Microwave or millimetre wave items

1. "Vacuum electronic devices" and cathodes:
   a. Travelling-wave devices: >31.8 GHz; cathode heater turn-on <3s; coupled cavity >7% bandwidth or >2.5 kW peak; helix/folded waveguide/serpentine with specific power-bandwidth-frequency combos, "space-qualified", or gridded electron gun; ≥10% bandwidth with annular/non-axisymmetric/multiple beams
   b. Crossed-field amplifiers: gain >17 dB
   c. Thermionic cathodes: >5 A/cm2 continuous or >10 A/cm2 pulsed
   d. "Vacuum electronic devices" with 'dual mode' capability

2. "MMIC" amplifiers (detailed power/frequency thresholds across 8 frequency bands from 2.7 GHz to >90 GHz)
   Note 2: Control determined by lowest peak saturated power output threshold when operating across multiple bands.

3. Discrete microwave transistors (detailed power/frequency thresholds across 6 frequency bands from 2.7 GHz to >43.5 GHz)

4. Microwave solid state amplifiers and assemblies/modules (detailed thresholds, 2.7 GHz to >90 GHz)

5. Electronically/magnetically tunable band-pass/band-stop filters (>5 tunable resonators, 1.5:1 band, <10μs tuning)

7. Converters and harmonic mixers (extending signal analysers >90 GHz, signal generators >90 GHz or >100mW at 43.5-90 GHz, network analysers >110 GHz, microwave test receivers >110 GHz)

8. Microwave power amplifiers with vacuum electronic devices (>3 GHz, >80 W/kg power-to-mass, <400 cm3)

9. Microwave power modules MPM (<10s turn-on, volume <power x 10 cm3/W, >1 octave bandwidth, RF output >100W at ≤18 GHz or >18 GHz)

10. Oscillators with SSB phase noise < -(126+20log10F-20log10f) dBc/Hz for 10 Hz ≤ F ≤ 10 kHz

11. 'Frequency synthesiser' assemblies with specific frequency switching times across various bands

12. 'Transmit/receive modules', 'transmit modules' and MMICs (>2.7 GHz, power/frequency/size/phase shifter requirements)
