### 1C350 — Chemicals as precursors for toxic agents (89 entries with CAS numbers)
Key entries include: thiodiglycol (CAS 111-48-8), phosphorus oxychloride (CAS 10025-87-3), methylphosphonyl dichloride (CAS 676-97-1), phosphorus trichloride (CAS 7719-12-2), thionyl chloride (CAS 7719-09-7), hydrogen fluoride (CAS 7664-39-3), potassium cyanide (CAS 151-50-8), sodium cyanide (CAS 143-33-9), and many others.
Notes on mixture thresholds: 10% or 30% weight thresholds depending on States Party/Non-Party to CWC and specific chemicals.
