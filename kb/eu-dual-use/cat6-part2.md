# Category 6 (Part 2) — Sensors and Lasers: Cameras, Optics, Lasers, Magnetometers, Gravity, Radar (6A003-6A226, 6B-6E)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

### 6A003 — Cameras, systems or equipment

a. Instrumentation cameras: electronic streak cameras (<50ns temporal resolution); electronic framing cameras (>1M frames/s); electronic cameras (<1μs shutter, >125 full frames/s)
b. Imaging cameras: 1. Video cameras (>4M pixels mono, >4M per array colour 3-array, >12M 1-array colour) with controlled mirrors/optical control/tracking data annotation; 2. Scanning cameras (>8192 elements per linear array, mechanical scanning); 3. Cameras with controlled image intensifier tubes; 4. Cameras with controlled focal plane arrays (extensive notes and exclusions for microbolometer cameras, automotive, industrial, medical applications)

### 6A004 — Optical equipment and components

a. Optical mirrors: 1. 'Deformable mirrors' (>10mm aperture, resonant frequency ≥750Hz + >200 actuators, or high LIDT); 2. Lightweight monolithic (<30 kg/m2, >10 kg); 3. Lightweight "composite"/foam (<30 kg/m2, >2 kg); 4. Mirrors for beam steering stages
b. ZnSe/ZnS optical components (>100 cm3 or >80mm diameter + >20mm thick) for 3000-25000nm
c. "Space-qualified" optical components (lightweighted <20% equivalent density; coated substrates; segmented mirrors >1m; "composite" components with CTE ≤5x10^-6/K)
d. Optical control: 1. For space-qualified components; 2. Beam steering mirror stages (>50mm, >±26mrad, >500Hz, <10μrad accuracy); resonator alignment (≥100Hz, <10μrad); 3. Gimbals (>5° slew, >100Hz, <200μrad pointing)
e. 'Aspheric optical elements' (>400mm aperture, roughness <1nm rms, CTE <3x10^-6/K)
f. Dynamic wavefront measuring equipment (≥1kHz frame rate, ≤λ/20 accuracy)

### 6A005 — "Lasers", components and optical equipment

a. Non-tunable CW lasers: detailed power thresholds across 10 wavelength bands (from <150nm to >2100nm). Key thresholds include: 975-1150nm single transverse mode >1000W or >500W with <40GHz spectral bandwidth; 975-1150nm multiple transverse mode >2kW or >1kW with >18% wall-plug efficiency.
b. Non-tunable pulsed lasers: detailed power/energy/duration thresholds across 10 wavelength bands. Key thresholds include: 975-1150nm with multiple sub-entries for pulse durations <1ps, 1ps-1ns, 1ns-1μs, >1μs.
c. Tunable lasers: thresholds for <600nm, 600-1400nm, >1400nm.
d. Other lasers: 1. Semiconductor lasers (individual single/multiple transverse mode, bars, stacked arrays with detailed power density thresholds); 2. CO lasers; 3. CO2 lasers; 4. Excimer lasers; 5. Chemical lasers (HF, DF, transfer lasers); 6. Non-repetitive pulsed Nd:glass.
e. Components: cooled mirrors; transmissive/partially transmissive components for specified lasers; fibre laser components (fused tapered combiners, MLDs for coherent beam combining).
f. Optical equipment: SHPL diagnostic, coherent beam combination for phased array SHPL, projection telescopes for SHPL.
g. 'Laser acoustic detection equipment' (CW ≥20mW, stability ≤10MHz, 1000-2000nm, resolution <1nm, SNR ≥10^3)

### 6A006 — "Magnetometers", "magnetic gradiometers", underwater electric field sensors, "compensation systems"
a. Magnetometers: SQUID (stationary ≤50 fT or in-motion ≤20 pT); optically pumped/nuclear precession (<20 pT); fluxgate (≤10 pT); induction coil (various thresholds by frequency); fibre optic (<1 nT)
b. Underwater electric field sensors (<8 nV/m/√Hz at 1Hz)
c. Magnetic gradiometers: using controlled magnetometers; fibre optic intrinsic (<0.3 nT/m); other intrinsic (<0.015 nT/m)
d. Compensation systems achieving 6A006.a/b/c performance

### 6A007 — Gravity meters and gravity gradiometers
a. Ground gravity meters (static accuracy <10 μGal)
b. Mobile gravity meters (static accuracy <0.7 mGal, operational accuracy <0.7 mGal, time-to-steady-state <2 min)
c. Gravity gradiometers

### 6A008 — Radar systems, equipment and assemblies
a. 40-230 GHz (>100mW average or locating accuracy ≤1m/0.2°)
b. Tunable bandwidth >±6.25% of centre frequency
c. Simultaneous operation on >2 carrier frequencies
d. SAR, ISAR, or SLAR mode capable
e. Electronically scanned array antennae
f. Height-finding of non-cooperative targets
g. Airborne with Doppler for moving targets
h. Radar spread spectrum or frequency agility
i. Ground-based instrumented range >185 km
j. Laser radar/LIDAR ("space-qualified"; coherent heterodyne/homodyne <20μrad; or airborne bathymetric to IHO Order 1a)
k. "Pulse compression" (ratio >150 or compressed pulse <200ns)
l. Data processing with 'automatic target tracking' or superposition/correlation from geographically dispersed sensors
