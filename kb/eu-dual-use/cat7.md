# Category 7 — Navigation and Avionics

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 7A — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 7A001 — Accelerometers and components
a. Linear accelerometers: 1. ≤15g with "bias" "stability" <130 micro g/year or "scale factor" "stability" <130 ppm/year; 2. >15g ≤100g with "bias" "repeatability" <1250 micro g/year AND "scale factor" "repeatability" <1250 ppm/year; 3. Designed for inertial navigation/guidance systems, >100g.
   Note: 7A001.a.1. and .a.2. do not control accelerometers limited to vibration/shock measurement.
b. Angular or rotational accelerometers, specified for >100g.

### 7A002 — Gyros or angular rate sensors and components
a. ≤100g: 1. <500°/s range with "bias" "stability" <0.5°/hr or "angle random walk" ≤0.0035°/sqrt(hr); 2. ≥500°/s range with "bias" "stability" <4°/hr or "angle random walk" ≤0.1°/sqrt(hr).
b. Specified for >100g.

### 7A003 — Inertial measurement equipment or systems
Note 1: Includes AHRS, gyrocompasses, IMUs, INS, IRS, IRUs.
Note 2: Does not control equipment certified for "civil aircraft".

a. Without 'positional aiding references': 1. ≤0.8 nm/hr CEP rate; 2. ≤0.5% distance CEP; or 3. ≤1 nm CEP in 24 hr.
b. With embedded 'positional aiding reference', <10m CEP after 4 min loss of all aids.
c. Heading/True North: 1. <500°/s max rate, heading accuracy ≤0.07° sec(Lat); 2. ≥500°/s, accuracy ≤0.2° sec(Lat).
d. Multi-axis acceleration/angular rate with performance per 7A001/7A002, or "space-qualified" with ARW ≤0.1°/sqrt(hr).

### 7A004 — Star trackers
a. Azimuth "accuracy" ≤20 arc seconds.
b. Components: optical heads, baffles, data processing units.

### 7A005 — Satellite navigation system receivers
a. Employing decryption for government ranging code.
b. Employing 'adaptive antenna systems'.

### 7A006 — Airborne altimeters (non 4.2-4.4 GHz) with 'power management' or phase shift key modulation.

### 7A008 — Underwater sonar navigation systems with positioning accuracy ≤3% distance CEP.

### 7A101-7A117 — MTCR-controlled items
7A101: Linear accelerometers for missiles, "bias" "repeatability" <1250 micro g, "scale factor" "repeatability" <1250 ppm.
7A102: Gyros for missiles, "drift rate" 'stability' <0.5°/hr.
7A103: Navigation equipment for missiles (inertial, flight instruments, integrated navigation ≤200m CEP, magnetic heading sensors).
7A104: Gyro-astro compasses for automatic celestial tracking.
7A105: Satellite navigation receivers for missiles/space launch vehicles.
7A106: Radar/laser altimeters for space launch vehicles.
7A115: Passive electromagnetic sensors for space launch vehicles.
7A116: Flight control systems and servo valves for missiles/space launch vehicles.
7A117: "Guidance sets" usable in "missiles" with ≤3.33% range accuracy (CEP ≤10km at 300km).

## 7B — Test, Inspection and Production Equipment
7B001: Test, calibration or alignment equipment for 7A (excluding Maintenance Level I/II).
7B002: Equipment for ring "laser" gyro mirrors (scatterometers ≤10 ppm, profilometers ≤0.5 nm).
7B003: "Production" equipment for 7A.
7B102: Reflectometers for "laser" gyro mirrors (≤50 ppm).
7B103: "Production facilities" for 7A117, other test/calibration equipment for 7A.

## 7C — None

## 7D — Software
7D001: For "development"/"production" of 7A or 7B.
7D002: "Source code" for operation/maintenance of inertial navigation equipment.
7D003: "Software" to improve performance to 7A003/7A004/7A008 levels, including hybrid integrated systems.
7D004: "Source code" for flight management, propulsion/flight control integration, fly-by-wire, fault-tolerant active flight control.
7D005: "Software" to decrypt "satellite navigation system" government-use ranging code.
7D101-7D104: MTCR software for controlled items.

## 7E — Technology
7E001: "Technology" for "development" of 7A, 7B, 7D equipment/"software".
7E002: "Technology" for "production" of 7A or 7B.
7E003: "Technology" for repair/refurbishing/overhaul of 7A001-7A004.
7E004: Other "technology" including: air data systems, 3D displays, electric actuators for primary flight control, active flight control systems, fly-by-wire/fly-by-light, helicopter systems, DBRN.
7E101-7E104: MTCR technology.
