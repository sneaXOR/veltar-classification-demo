# Category 3 (Part 2) — Electronics: Other Items (3A001.c-i, 3A002-3A234, 3B-3E)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 3A001 (continued)

### 3A001.c — Acoustic wave devices
1. Surface acoustic wave/surface skimming: carrier frequency >6 GHz; or carrier 1-6 GHz with specific side-lobe/bandwidth/delay specs; or carrier ≤1 GHz with specific bandwidth/delay/side-lobe specs
2. Bulk acoustic wave devices processing signals >6 GHz
3. Acoustic-optic signal processing devices

### 3A001.d — Superconductive electronic devices (current switching <10^-14 J delay-power product; resonant circuits Q>10,000)

### 3A001.e — High energy devices
1. Cells: Primary cells (energy density >550 Wh/kg with >50 W/kg, or >50 Wh/kg with >350 W/kg); Secondary cells (energy density >350 Wh/kg). Note: does not control batteries.
2. High energy storage capacitors: single shot (≥5kV, ≥250 J/kg, ≥25 kJ); repetition rated (≥5kV, ≥50 J/kg, ≥100 J, ≥10,000 cycles)
3. "Superconductive" electromagnets/solenoids (<1s charge/discharge, >10 kJ in first second, ID >250mm, >8T or >300 A/mm2)
4. "Space-qualified" solar cells/panels/arrays (>20% efficiency at 301K under AM0 1367 W/m2)

### 3A001.f — Rotary absolute position encoders (accuracy ≤1.0 arcsec)
### 3A001.g — Solid-state pulsed power switching thyristors (di/dt >30,000 A/μs at >1100V; or >2,000 A/μs at ≥3000V and ≥3000A peak)
### 3A001.h — Solid-state power semiconductor switches/diodes/'modules' (junction temp >488K, blocking voltage >300V, continuous current >1A)
### 3A001.i — Electro-optic modulators for analogue signals (10-20 GHz with Vπ <2.7V/4V; or ≥20 GHz with Vπ <3.3V/5V)

## 3A002 — General purpose electronic assemblies, modules and equipment

a.6. Digital data recorders (>6.4 Gbit/s to disk/SSD with signal processing while recording)
a.7. Real-time oscilloscopes (<2% rms noise at ≥60 GHz bandwidth per channel)

c. "Signal analysers": 1. RBW >40 MHz at 31.8-37 GHz; 2. DANL <-150 dBm/Hz at 43.5-90 GHz; 3. Frequency >90 GHz; 4. 'Real-time bandwidth' >170 MHz with 100% probability of discovery for ≤15μs signals

d. Signal generators: pulse-modulated at 31.8-37 GHz (<25ns pulse, ≥65 dB on/off); power >100 mW at 43.5-90 GHz; specific frequency switching times; SSB phase noise specs; 'RF modulation bandwidth' specs; or maximum frequency >90 GHz

e. Network analysers: >31.62 mW at 43.5-90 GHz; >1 mW at 90-110 GHz; 'nonlinear vector measurement' at 50-110 GHz; or >110 GHz

f. Microwave test receivers: >110 GHz with simultaneous amplitude and phase measurement

g. Atomic frequency standards: "space-qualified"; non-rubidium with stability <1x10^-11/month; or rubidium with <1x10^-11/month and <1W total power

h. Electronic assemblies/modules/equipment performing ADC (same thresholds as 3A001.a.14.) with output/storage/processing of digitised data
