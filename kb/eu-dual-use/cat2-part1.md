# Category 2 (Part 1) — Materials Processing: Systems, Equipment, Machine Tools (2A, 2B001-2B009)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

Technical Notes for 2B:
1. Secondary parallel contouring axes are not counted in total contouring axes. Rotary axes need not rotate over 360°.
2. Number of axes coordinated simultaneously for "contouring control" is the number along or around which simultaneous and interrelated motions are performed between workpiece and tool during processing.
3. Axis nomenclature per ISO 841:2001.
4. A "tilting spindle" is counted as a rotary axis.
5. 'Stated "unidirectional positioning repeatability"' may be used as alternative to individual machine tests (select 5 machines, measure per ISO 230-2:2014, calculate arithmetic mean).
6. Measurement uncertainty not considered for 2B001.a. to 2B001.c.
7. Measurement of axes per ISO 230-2:2014 test procedures 5.3.2. Tests for axes >2m made over 2m segments.

### 2A001 — Anti-friction bearings, bearing systems and components
a. Ball/solid roller bearings, ISO 492 Class 4/Class 2 or better, both 'rings' and 'rolling elements' made from monel or beryllium. Note: does not control tapered roller bearings.

## 2B — Test, Inspection and Production Equipment

### 2B001 — Machine tools for removing metals/ceramics/"composites" (NC-equippable)

Note 1: Does not control special purpose machines limited to gear manufacture (see 2B003).
Note 2: Does not control special purpose machines limited to: crankshafts/camshafts, tools/cutters, extruder worms, jewellery parts, dental prostheses.
Note 3: Machine with ≥2 of turning/milling/grinding must be evaluated against each applicable entry.
Note 4: Machine with additive manufacturing in addition to turning/milling/grinding must be evaluated against each applicable entry.

a. Turning machines (≥2 axes coordinated for contouring): 1. "Unidirectional positioning repeatability" ≤0.9μm (travel <1.0m); or 2. ≤1.1μm (travel ≥1.0m).
   Note 1: Does not control contact lens turning machines. Note 2: Does not control bar machines (Swissturn) ≤42mm bar, no chucks.

b. Milling machines:
   1. 3 linear + 1 rotary axes coordinated for contouring: ≤0.9μm (<1.0m) or ≤1.1μm (≥1.0m)
   2. ≥5 axes coordinated for contouring: ≤0.9μm (<1.0m), ≤1.4μm (≥1m <4m), or ≤6.0μm (≥4m)
   3. Jig boring machines: ≤1.1μm
   4. Fly cutting machines: spindle run-out <0.0004mm TIR, angular deviation <2 arcsec TIR over 300mm

c. Grinding machines:
   1. ≤1.1μm repeatability AND 3-4 axes for contouring; or
   2. ≥5 axes for contouring: ≤1.1μm (<1m), ≤1.4μm (≥1m <4m), or ≤6.0μm (≥4m)
   Note: does not control certain cylindrical grinders, jig grinders without z/w axis, or surface grinders.

d. EDM (non-wire type) with ≥2 rotary axes for contouring.

e. Machine tools using water/electron beam/"laser" beam with ≥2 rotary axes (coordinated for contouring, positioning accuracy <0.003°).

f. Deep-hole-drilling machines/turning machines modified for deep-hole-drilling, max depth-of-bore >5m.

### 2B005 — Equipment for inorganic overlays/coatings/surface modifications
a. CVD (pulsating/CNTD/plasma enhanced, with vacuum rotating seals or in-situ thickness control)
b. Ion implantation (beam current ≥5 mA)
c. EB-PVD (>80 kW, with laser pool level control or luminescence rate monitor)
d. Plasma spraying (reduced pressure ≤10 kPa or in-situ thickness control)
e. Sputter deposition (≥0.1 mA/mm2, ≥15μm/h)
f. Cathodic arc deposition (with electromagnetic grid for arc spot steering)
g. Ion plating (in-situ measurement of optical characteristics or thickness)

### 2B006 — Dimensional inspection/measuring systems
a. CMM with volumetric E0,MPE ≤(1.7+L/1000)μm per ISO 10360-2:2009
b. Linear displacement: 1. Non-contact ≤0.2μm within 0-0.2mm; 2. Linear feedback ≤(800+600xL/1000)nm; 3. Laser systems ≤0.200nm resolution AND ≤(1.6+L/2000)nm uncertainty; 4. Electronic assemblies for feedback
c. Rotary position feedback ≤0.9 arcsec accuracy
d. Surface roughness measurement (optical scatter, sensitivity ≤0.5nm)
