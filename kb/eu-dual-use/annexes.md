# EU Dual-Use Regulation — Annexes II to VI

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821

---

## ANNEX II — Union General Export Authorisations (EU001 to EU008)

### EU001 — Exports to Australia, Canada, Iceland, Japan, New Zealand, Norway, Switzerland (incl. Liechtenstein), United Kingdom, United States

Items: All dual-use items in Annex I EXCEPT those listed in Section I of Annex II.
Conditions: Not authorised if items intended for WMD, military end-use in embargoed country, or parts of military items exported without authorisation. Not for customs-free zones.
Notification of first use within 30 days or prior to first use.

### EU002 — Exports of certain dual-use items to Argentina, South Africa, South Korea, Turkey

Items (limited list): 1A001, 1A003, 1A004, 1C003.b., 1C003.c., 1C004, 1C005, 1C006, 1C008, 1C009, 2B008, 3A001.a.3., 3A001.a.6., 3A001.a.7., 3A001.a.9., 3A001.a.10., 3A001.a.11., 3A001.a.12., 3A002.c., 3A002.d., 3A002.e., 3A002.f., 3C001, 3C002, 3C003, 3C004, 3C005, 3C006.
Same conditions as EU001.

### EU003 — Export after repair/replacement

Items: All Annex I except Section I items, all D and E sections, and specific exclusions (1A002.a., 1C012.a., 1C227-1C240, 1C350, 1C450, 5A001.b.5., 5A002.c./d./e., 5A003.a./b., 6A001.a.2.a.1./a.2.a.5., 6A002.a.1.c., 8A001.b./c.1., 9A011).
Destinations: Albania, Argentina, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Brazil, Chile, China (incl. HK/Macao), French overseas territories, India, Kazakhstan, Mexico, Montenegro, Morocco, North Macedonia, Russia, Serbia, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Tunisia, Turkey, Ukraine, UAE.
Conditions: Only when initial export used UGEA or had authorisation, valid only to original end-user, within 5 years of original authorisation.

### EU004 — Temporary export for exhibition or fair

Items: All Annex I except Section I, all D and E sections, and specific exclusions.
Destinations: Same as EU003.
Conditions: Temporary export for exhibition/fair, reimported within 120 days, complete and without modification. Extensive additional conditions (no classified items, no private demonstrations, no production integration, no transfer of technology).

### EU005 — Telecommunications

Items: 5A001.b.2., 5A001.c., 5A001.d. (and related test/production equipment and software); 5E001.a. technology for installation/operation/maintenance/repair.
Destinations: Argentina, China (incl. HK/Macao), India, South Africa, South Korea, Russia, Turkey, Ukraine.
Additional condition: Not for use in connection with human rights violations via interception/surveillance technologies.

### EU006 — Chemicals

Items: Specific entries from 1C350 (65 chemicals with CAS numbers) and 1C450.a.4-7, 1C450.b.1-6, 1C450.b.8.
Destinations: Argentina, South Korea, Turkey, Ukraine.
Same conditions as EU001 plus no re-export to non-listed destinations.

### EU007 — Intra-group export of software and technology

Items: All technology and software in Annex I EXCEPT Section I items and items related to 4A005, 4D004, 4E001.c, 5A001.f, 5A001.j.
Destinations: Argentina, Brazil, Chile, India, Indonesia, Israel, Jordan, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco, Philippines, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Thailand, Tunisia.
Conditions: Only for wholly-owned subsidiaries or sister companies; parent must be in EU or EU001 country; ICP required; software/technology for commercial product development only; must remain under exporter/parent control; returned/deleted when development complete.

### EU008 — Encryption

Items: 5A002.a.2, 5A002.a.3, 5A002.b. (cryptographic activation tokens), 5D002.a.1./b./c.1. (related software), 5E002.b. (related technology).
Conditions: Only published/commercial cryptographic standards, no government-use standards, no user-modifiable functionality, not accredited for classified information.
Destinations: All EXCEPT EU001 destinations and a specific exclusion list (Afghanistan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Cambodia, CAR, China, Congo, DRC, Egypt, Eritrea, Georgia, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Lebanon, Libya, Malaysia, Mali, Mauritius, Mongolia, Myanmar, North Korea, Oman, Pakistan, Qatar, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, UAE, Uzbekistan, Venezuela, Yemen, Zimbabwe, and any destination under arms embargo or EU dual-use restrictive measures).
Registration and technical data submission required.

## ANNEX IV — List of dual-use items subject to intra-EU transfer controls (Article 11(1))

### Part I — Possibility of National General Authorisation for intra-Union trade

Items of stealth technology: 1C001, 1C101, 1D103, 1E101, 1E102, 6B008, 6B108

Items of EU strategic control: 1A007, 1C239, 1E201, 3A229, 3A232, 3E201, 6A001 (specific acoustic entries), 6D003.a, 8A002.o.3, 8E002.a

Items of EU strategic control — Cryptanalysis: 5A004.a, 5D002.a.3, 5D002.c.3, 5E002.a (only for 5A004.a/5D002.a.3/5D002.c.3)

Items of MTCR technology: 7A117, 7B001/7B003/7B103 (for 7A117), 7D101/7E001/7E002/7E101 (for 7A117), 9A004, 9A005, 9A007.a, 9A008.d, 9A104, 9A105.a, 9A106.c, 9A108.c, 9A116, 9A119, 9B115/9B116 (for MTCR items), 9D101/9E001/9E002/9E101/9E102 (for MTCR items)

Exemptions: Items transferred by/for ESA, national space organisations, EU space launch development programmes, or to State-controlled launching sites in a Member State.

### Part II — No National General Authorisation for intra-Union trade

CWC items: 1C351.d.4 (Ricin), 1C351.d.5 (Saxitoxin)

NSG technology: All Category 0 (with exceptions for 0C001 and most of 0C002), plus: 1B226, 1B231, 1B233, 1C012.b, 1C233, 1C235, 1E001/1E201 (for NSG items), 3A228, 3A231, 3E201 (for NSG items), 6A203.a/b (mechanical cameras only), 6A225, 6A226
