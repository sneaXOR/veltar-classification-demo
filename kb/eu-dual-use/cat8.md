# Category 8 — Marine

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 8A — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 8A001 — Submersible vehicles and surface vessels

a. Manned, tethered submersible vehicles designed for depths >1000m
b. Manned, untethered submersible vehicles: 1. Designed to 'operate autonomously' with lifting ≥10% weight in air AND ≥15 kN; 2. Designed for depths >1000m; or 3. Continuously autonomous ≥10 hrs and range ≥25 nm.
c. Unmanned submersible vehicles: 1. With autonomous course-setting, acoustic data/command link, or optical link >1000m; 2. Tethered, >1000m depth, self-propelled with controlled motors/thrusters per 8A002.a.2. or fibre optic data link.
e. Ocean salvage systems >5 MN capacity, depths >250m, with dynamic positioning ≤20m or seafloor navigation >1000m with ≤10m accuracy.

### 8A002 — Marine systems, equipment and components

a. For submersible vehicles, designed for >1000m: 1. Pressure housings >1.5m diameter; 2. DC propulsion motors; 3. Umbilical cables with optical fibre; 4. Components from 8C001 materials.
b. Automated motion control systems: position keeping ≤10m, cable-following ≤10m.
d. Underwater vision systems with range-gated illumination or range-gated laser.
h. "Robots" for underwater use with force/torque sensing or ≥250N force with titanium/"composite" structural members.
j. Air independent power systems for underwater: 1. Brayton/Rankine cycle; 2. Diesel cycle; 3. "Fuel cell" >2kW; 4. Stirling cycle.
o. Propulsion systems: controllable-pitch propellers >30MW; electric motors >2.5MW; "superconductive" motors >0.1MW; "composite" transmission shafts >2MW; ventilated propeller systems >2.5MW.
o.3. Noise reduction systems for vessels ≥1000 tonnes: compound acoustic mounts >30% intermediate mass; active noise reduction/cancellation systems for power transmission.
p. Pumpjet propulsion systems >2.5MW with divergent nozzle/flow conditioning.
q. Underwater swimming/diving equipment: closed circuit rebreathers; semi-closed circuit rebreathers.
r. Diver deterrent acoustic systems ≥190 dB at ≤200 Hz.
