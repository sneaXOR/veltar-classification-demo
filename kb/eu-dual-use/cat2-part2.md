# Category 2 (Part 2) — Materials Processing: MTCR/NSG/CWC Equipment (2B104-2B352), Software, Technology

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 2B (continued) — MTCR/NSG/CWC Equipment

### 2B104 — "Isostatic presses" (non-2B004): ≥69 MPa, ≥873K, cavity ID ≥254mm
### 2B105 — CVD furnaces for densification of carbon-carbon composites
### 2B109 — Flow-forming machines for "missile" propulsion components (NC-equippable, >2 axes for contouring)
### 2B116 — Vibration test systems (≥10g rms 20Hz-2kHz, ≥50 kN 'bare table'), digital controllers (>5 kHz real-time bandwidth), vibration thrusters (≥50 kN), combining structures (≥50 kN combined)
### 2B117 — Equipment for densification/pyrolysis of composite rocket nozzles and reentry vehicle nose tips
### 2B119 — Balancing machines (mass ≤3kg, speed >12,500 rpm, 2+ planes, ≤0.2 g·mm/kg residual unbalance)
### 2B120 — Motion simulators/rate tables (≥2 axes, with slip rings, various rate/accuracy specs)
### 2B121 — Positioning tables (≥2 axes, accuracy ≤5 arcsec)
### 2B122 — Centrifuges (>100g, with slip rings or non-contact devices)

### 2B201 — Machine tools (non-2B001) for metals/ceramics/"composites" with simultaneous contouring
a. Milling: positioning accuracy ≤6μm per ISO 230-2:1988, or ≥2 contouring rotary axes, or ≥5 axes for contouring
b. Grinding: positioning accuracy ≤4μm, or ≥2 contouring rotary axes, or ≥5 axes for contouring
c. Turning: positioning accuracy <6μm for workpiece >35mm diameter

### 2B204 — "Isostatic presses" (non-2B004/2B104): ≥69 MPa, cavity ID >152mm; and dies/moulds/controls
### 2B206 — Dimensional inspection machines (non-2B006): CMMs, hemishell inspection systems, laser linear displacement systems, LVDTs
### 2B207 — "Robots"/"end-effectors" for high explosives handling
### 2B209 — Flow forming/spin forming machines (≥3 rollers, NC-equippable) and mandrels (75-400mm ID)
### 2B219 — Centrifugal multiplane balancing machines for flexible rotors and hollow cylindrical rotors

### 2B225 — Remote manipulators for radiochemical operations (penetrating ≥0.6m wall or bridging over)
### 2B226 — Controlled atmosphere induction furnaces (>1123K, coils ≤600mm, ≥5kW) and power supplies
### 2B227 — Vacuum metallurgical melting/casting furnaces (arc remelt, electron beam, plasma) and related equipment
### 2B228 — Rotor fabrication/assembly equipment, straightening equipment, bellows-forming mandrels/dies for gas centrifuges
### 2B230 — Pressure transducers (absolute pressure, Al/Ni/fluoropolymer sensing elements, <13 kPa with accuracy <1% or ≥13 kPa with accuracy <130 Pa)
### 2B231 — Vacuum pumps (throat ≥380mm, speed ≥15 m3/s, ultimate vacuum <13 mPa)
### 2B232 — High-velocity gun systems (projectiles ≥1.5 km/s)
### 2B233 — Bellows-sealed scroll compressors/vacuum pumps (≥50 m3/h, pressure ratio ≥2:1, corrosion-resistant materials)

### 2B350 — Chemical manufacturing facilities, equipment and components
a. Reaction vessels 0.1-20 m3 (corrosion-resistant materials)
b. Agitators for 2B350.a. reaction vessels
c. Storage tanks >0.1 m3 (corrosion-resistant materials)
d. Heat exchangers 0.15-20 m2 (corrosion-resistant materials)
e. Distillation/absorption columns ID >0.1m
f. Remotely operated filling equipment
g. Valves and components (various nominal sizes, corrosion-resistant materials)
h. Multi-walled piping with leak detection
i. Multiple-seal/seal-less pumps (>0.6 m3/h) or vacuum pumps (>5 m3/h)
j. Incinerators for 1C350 chemicals (>1273K combustion chamber)
k. Prefabricated repair assemblies (tantalum/tantalum alloys)

'Corrosion resistant materials' for 2B350.g: Ni >40%, alloys >25% Ni and 20% Cr, fluoropolymers >35% F, glass/glass-lined, Ta, Ti, Zr, Nb alloys, and specific ceramics.

### 2B351 — Toxic gas monitors (<0.3 mg/m3 for CW agents or cholinesterase-inhibiting activity detection)

### 2B352 — Biological manufacturing and handling equipment
a. P3/P4 containment facilities and equipment (autoclaves, breathing air suit showers, mechanical-seal doors)
b. Fermenters ≥20 litres (capable of cultivating microorganisms/live cells without aerosol propagation)
c. Centrifugal separators (>100 l/hr, stainless steel/titanium, in-situ steam sterilisation)
d. Cross-flow filtration equipment (≥1 m2 area, sterilisable/disposable) and components (≥0.2 m2)
e. Freeze-drying equipment (10-1000 kg ice/24h, steam/gas/vapour sterilisable)
f. Protective equipment (positive pressure suits, biocontainment chambers/isolators/biosafety cabinets)
g. Aerosol inhalation equipment (whole-body ≥1 m3, nose-only for ≥12 rodents or ≥2 animals)
h. Spray drying equipment (0.4-400 kg/h water evaporation, ≤10μm particle size, sterilisable)
i. Nucleic acid assemblers/synthesisers (automated, >1.5 kilobases, error rate <5%)
