# Category 9 (Part 2) — Aerospace and Propulsion: MTCR Items (9A101-9A350) and 9B-9E

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 9A (continued) — MTCR-Controlled Items

### 9A101 — Turbojet and turbofan engines (non-9A001)
a. Engines with: 'maximum thrust value' >400N (excluding civil certified >8,890N); SFC ≤0.15 kg/N/h; 'dry weight' <750 kg; 'first-stage rotor diameter' <1m.
b. Engines designed for "missiles" or UAVs per 9A012/9A112.a.

### 9A102 — 'Turboprop engine systems' for UAVs (9A012/9A112.a), 'maximum power' >10kW. Not civil certified.

### 9A104 — Sounding rockets capable of range ≥300 km.

### 9A105 — Liquid/gel propellant rocket engines for "missiles" (≥1.1 MNs) or for systems capable of 300km range (≥0.841 MNs).

### 9A106 — Components for liquid rocket propulsion: thrust vector control, propellant control systems, combustion chambers/nozzles.

### 9A107 — Solid propellant rocket motors for 300km-capable systems (≥0.841 MNs).

### 9A108 — Components for solid/hybrid rocket propulsion: motor cases, nozzles, thrust vector control for "missiles".

### 9A109 — Hybrid rocket motors for 300km-capable systems (≥0.841 MNs).

### 9A110 — Composite structures for 'missiles' or missile subsystems.

### 9A111 — Pulse jet or detonation engines for "missiles" or UAVs.

### 9A112 — "UAVs" (non-9A012): a. capable of 300km range; b. with autonomous navigation or BVLOS and aerosol dispensing >20 litres.

### 9A115 — Launch support equipment for space launch vehicles, sounding rockets, or 'missiles'.
### 9A116 — Reentry vehicles for "missiles" (heat shields, heat sinks, electronic equipment).
### 9A117 — Staging mechanisms, separation mechanisms, interstages for "missiles".
### 9A118 — Combustion regulation devices for engines in "missiles" or UAVs.
### 9A119 — Individual rocket stages for 300km-capable systems.
### 9A120 — Liquid/gel propellant tanks for 300km/500kg systems.
### 9A121 — Umbilical and interstage electrical connectors for "missiles"/space launch vehicles.
### 9A350 — Spraying/fogging systems for aircraft/UAVs with VMD <50μm, flow rate >2 l/min.

## 9B — Test, Inspection and Production Equipment

9B001: Manufacturing equipment for superalloy turbine components (directional solidification, single crystal casting, additive manufacturing).
9B002: On-line control systems for gas turbine engine development.
9B003: Equipment for gas turbine brush seals (>335 m/s, >773K).
9B004: Tools/dies/fixtures for superalloy/titanium/intermetallic airfoil-to-disk combinations.
9B005: On-line control systems for: wind tunnels ≥Mach 1.2; flow simulation >Mach 5; wind tunnels with Reynolds >25x10^6.
9B006: Acoustic vibration test equipment ≥160 dB, ≥4 kW, >1273K.
9B007: Equipment for rocket motor integrity inspection (NDT).
9B009: Tooling for powder metallurgy gas turbine rotors (≥60% UTS at ≥873K).
9B010: Equipment for production of 9A012 items.
9B105: 'Aerodynamic test facilities' for ≥Mach 0.9, usable for 'missiles'.
9B106: Environmental chambers and anechoic chambers.
9B107: 'Aerothermodynamic test facilities' for 'missiles' (≥5 MW or ≥3 MPa).
9B115-9B117: Production equipment and facilities for controlled propulsion systems.

## 9C — Materials

9C108: "Insulation" material for rocket motor cases.
9C110: Resin impregnated fibre prepregs for composite structures (specific tensile strength >7.62x10^4 m, specific modulus >3.18x10^6 m, Tg >418K).

## 9D — Software

9D001: For "development" of 9A001-9A119, 9B or 9E003.
9D002: For "production" of 9A001-9A119 or 9B.
9D003: "Software" incorporating 9E003.h. "technology" for "FADEC Systems".
9D004: Viscous flow software, gas turbine test software, directional solidification control, UAV operation software, turbine cooling design software, aero thermal prediction software.
9D005: For operation of 9A004.e. or 9A004.f.
9D101-9D105: MTCR software for controlled items.

## 9E — Technology

9E001: For "development" of 9A001.b., 9A004-9A012, 9A350, 9B or 9D.
9E002: For "production" of 9A001.b., 9A004-9A011, 9A350 or 9B.
9E003: Other "technology" for: (a) gas turbine components (DS/SC alloys, combustors, composites, cooled/uncooled turbine blades, airfoil-to-disk, powder metallurgy, fan blades); (b) wind tunnel models, composite propeller blades; (c) cooling holes; (d) helicopter power transfer; (e-g) high output diesel engines; (h) FADEC systems; (i) adjustable flow path systems; (j) wing-folding systems.
9E101-9E102: MTCR technology.
