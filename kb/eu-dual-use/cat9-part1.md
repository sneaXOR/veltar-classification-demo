# Category 9 (Part 1) — Aerospace and Propulsion: Systems and Equipment (9A001-9A012)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 9A — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 9A001 — Aero gas turbine engines
a. Incorporating "technologies" specified in 9E003.a., 9E003.h. or 9E003.i.
   Note 1: Does not control engines certified for civil aviation AND intended to power non-military manned "aircraft" with civil type certificate.
   Note 2: Does not control engines designed for APUs approved by civil aviation authority.
b. Designed to power an "aircraft" to cruise at Mach 1 or more, for more than 30 minutes.

### 9A002 — 'Marine gas turbine engines' (liquid fuel)
a. Maximum continuous power ≥24,245 kW at ISO 3977-2:1997 conditions; and
b. 'Corrected specific fuel consumption' ≤0.219 kg/kWh at 35% max continuous power.

### 9A003 — Assemblies/components incorporating 9E003.a./h./i. "technologies" for aero gas turbine engines specified in 9A001 or of non-EU/non-WA origin.

### 9A004 — Space launch vehicles, "spacecraft", "spacecraft buses", "spacecraft payloads", on-board systems, terrestrial equipment, air-launch platforms
a. Space launch vehicles
b. "Spacecraft"
c. "Spacecraft buses"
d. "Spacecraft payloads" incorporating items from 3A001.b.1.a.4., 3A002.g., 5A001.a.1., 5A001.b.3., 5A002.c., 5A002.e., 6A002.a.1., 6A002.a.2., 6A002.b., 6A002.d., 6A003.b., 6A004.c., 6A004.e., 6A008.d., 6A008.e., 6A008.k., 6A008.l. or 9A010.c.
e. On-board systems for: 1. Command and telemetry data handling; 2. Payload data handling; 3. Attitude and orbit control.
f. Terrestrial equipment: 1. Telemetry and telecommand; 2. Simulators for verification of operational procedures.
g. "Aircraft" specially designed as air-launch platforms for space launch vehicles.
h. "Sub-orbital craft".

### 9A005 — Liquid rocket propulsion systems containing systems/components specified in 9A006.
### 9A006 — Systems and components for liquid rocket propulsion (cryogenic, turbo pumps, thrust chambers, propellant storage, injectors, carbon-carbon chambers).
### 9A007 — Solid rocket propulsion systems (>1.1 MNs impulse, ≥2.4 kNs/kg specific impulse, >88% mass fraction, components per 9A008, or insulation/bonding with 'strong mechanical bond').
### 9A008 — Components for solid rocket propulsion (insulation/bonding, filament-wound cases >0.61m, nozzles >45kN, movable nozzle TVC systems).
### 9A009 — Hybrid rocket propulsion (>1.1 MNs impulse or >220 kN thrust in vacuum).
### 9A010 — Specially designed components/structures for launch vehicles/"spacecraft" (composite structures >10kg, structural components, pulsed liquid rocket engines with thrust-to-weight ≥1 kN/kg and response <30ms).
### 9A011 — Ramjet, scramjet or 'combined cycle engines' and components.

### 9A012 — "UAVs", unmanned "airships", related equipment and components
a. "UAVs" or unmanned "airships" designed for controlled flight out of direct 'natural vision' with: 1. endurance ≥30min but <1hr AND designed for wind gusts ≥46.3 km/h; or 2. endurance ≥1 hour.
b. Related equipment: 3. Equipment to convert manned aircraft to UAV; 4. Air breathing engines for UAVs at altitudes >15,240m.
