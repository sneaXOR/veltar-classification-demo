# Category 1 (Part 2) — Special Materials: Materials (1C001-1C012)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

Technical Note (Metals and alloys): Unless provision to the contrary is made, 'metals' and 'alloys' in 1C001 to 1C012 cover crude and semi-fabricated forms. Crude forms include: anodes, balls, bars, billets, blocks, etc. Semi-fabricated forms include wrought/worked materials and cast materials.

## 1C — Materials

### 1C001 — Materials for absorbing electromagnetic radiation, or intrinsically conductive polymers
a. For frequencies >2x10^8 Hz to <3x10^12 Hz (with extensive exclusion notes for various absorber types)
b. Materials not transparent to visible light, for absorbing near-infrared 810-2000 nm
c. Intrinsically conductive polymeric materials with 'bulk electrical conductivity' >10,000 S/m (polyaniline, polypyrrole, polythiophene, polyphenylene-vinylene, polythienylene-vinylene)

### 1C002 — Metal alloys, powder and alloyed materials
Note: Does not control materials specially formulated for coating purposes.
a. Aluminides: 1. Nickel aluminides (≥15% Al, ≤38% Al, + alloying element); 2. Titanium aluminides (≥10% Al + alloying element)
b. Metal alloys from 1C002.c. powder: Ni alloys (stress-rupture/low cycle fatigue specs), Nb alloys, Ti alloys, Al alloys (tensile strength ≥240 MPa at 473K or ≥415 MPa at 298K), Mg alloys (≥345 MPa tensile, <1 mm/yr corrosion)
c. Metal alloy powder/particulate (Ni-Al-X, Nb-Al-X, Ti-Al-X, Al-Mg-X, Mg-Al-X systems, made by vacuum/gas/rotary atomisation, splat quenching, melt spinning/extraction, mechanical alloying, plasma atomisation)
d. Alloyed materials (uncomminuted flakes/ribbons/thin rods from 1C002.c.1. compositions)

### 1C003 — Magnetic metals
a. Initial relative permeability ≥120,000, thickness ≤0.05mm
b. Magnetostrictive alloys (saturation magnetostriction >5x10^-4 or coupling factor k >0.8)
c. Amorphous or 'nanocrystalline' alloy strips (≥75% Fe/Co/Ni, Bs ≥1.6T, thickness ≤0.02mm or resistivity ≥2x10^-4 ohm cm)

### 1C004 — Uranium titanium alloys or tungsten alloys (density >17.5 g/cm3, elastic limit >880 MPa, UTS >1270 MPa, elongation >8%)

### 1C005 — "Superconductive" "composite" conductors (>100m or >100g)
a. Nb-Ti filaments in non-copper matrix, <0.28x10^-4 mm2 cross-section
b. Other superconductors with critical temp >9.85K, remaining superconductive at 4.2K in 12T with current density >1750 A/mm2
c. Filaments remaining superconductive above 115K

### 1C006 — Fluids and lubricating materials (fluorinated silicone fluids, damping/flotation fluids, fluorocarbon electronic cooling fluids)

### 1C007 — Ceramic powders, ceramic-"matrix" "composite" materials and 'precursor materials'
a. TiB2 powder (<5000 ppm metallic impurities, ≤5μm average, ≤10% >10μm)
c. Ceramic-"matrix" "composites" (glass/oxide matrix with Al2O3 or Si-C-N fibres; carbide/nitride matrix of Si, Zr, B)
e. 'Precursor materials' (polydiorganosilanes, polysilazanes, polycarbosilazanes)

### 1C008 — Non-fluorinated polymeric substances
a. Imides: 1. Bismaleimides; 2. Aromatic PAI (Tg >563K); 3. Aromatic polyimides (Tg >505K); 4. Aromatic polyetherimides (Tg >563K)
d. Polyarylene ketones
e. Polyarylene sulphides (biphenylene/triphenylene)
f. Polybiphenylenethersulphone (Tg >563K)

### 1C009 — Unprocessed fluorinated compounds
b. Fluorinated polyimides ≥10% wt combined fluorine
c. Fluorinated phosphazene elastomers ≥30% wt combined fluorine

### 1C010 — "Fibrous or filamentary materials"
a. Organic: "specific modulus" >12.7x10^6 m AND "specific tensile strength" >23.5x10^4 m (excl. polyethylene)
b. Carbon: "specific modulus" >14.65x10^6 m AND "specific tensile strength" >26.82x10^4 m
c. Inorganic: (≥50% SiO2, specific modulus >2.54x10^6m; or other, >5.6x10^6m) AND melting/decomposition >1922K
d. Fibrous materials composed of 1C008.a. polyetherimides or 1C008.d.-f. materials, or 'commingled' with other controlled fibres
e. Prepregs/preforms/'carbon fibre preforms' with controlled fibrous materials and controlled resins/pitch

### 1C011 — Metals and compounds
a. Zr, Mg and alloys, particle size <60μm, ≥99% purity
b. Boron or boron alloys <60μm (boron ≥85%)
c. Guanidine nitrate (CAS 506-93-4)
d. Nitroguanidine (NQ) (CAS 556-88-7)

### 1C012 — Nuclear heat source materials
a. Plutonium (Pu-238 >50% isotopic assay)
b. "Previously separated" neptunium-237
