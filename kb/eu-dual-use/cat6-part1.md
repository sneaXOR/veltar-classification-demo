# Category 6 (Part 1) — Sensors and Lasers: Acoustic Systems and Optical Sensors (6A001-6A002)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 6A — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 6A001 — Acoustic systems, equipment and components

#### 6A001.a — Marine acoustic systems

a.1. Active systems:
   a.1.a. Acoustic seabed survey equipment: 1. Surface vessel (>20° angle, >600m depth, sounding resolution <2, enhanced depth accuracy); 2. Underwater survey (>300m with sounding rate >3800 m/s; or >100m with >20° angle + <350kHz or >200m range + enhanced depth accuracy); 3. Side Scan Sonar/SAS (>500m depth, area coverage rate >570 m2/s at <15cm along/across track resolution)
   a.1.b. Object detection/location: transmitting <10kHz; SPL >224dB at 10-24kHz; SPL >235dB at 24-30kHz; beams <1° at <100kHz; unambiguous range >5120m; or >1000m depth with dynamic compensation or non-PZT transducers
   a.1.c. Acoustic projectors: <10kHz (non-continuous >10log(f)+169.77 dB SL or continuous >10log(f)+159.77 dB SL); or side-lobe suppression >22 dB
   a.1.d. Acoustic positioning systems (detection >1000m, position error <10m rms at 1000m)
   a.1.e. Active diver/swimmer detection sonars (detection >530m, position error <15m rms at 530m, bandwidth >3kHz)

a.2. Passive systems:
   a.2.a. Hydrophones: continuous flexible sensing; flexible assemblies <20mm spacing; optical fibres, specific piezoelectric materials, PMN-PT/PIN-PMN-PT single crystals; sensitivity better than -180dB (no acceleration compensation); designed for >35m with acceleration compensation; or >1000m with sensitivity better than -230dB below 4kHz
   a.2.b. Towed acoustic hydrophone arrays: group spacing <12.5m or 'able to be modified'; designed for >35m or 'able to be modified'; heading sensors per 6A001.a.2.d.; longitudinally reinforced; <40mm diameter; or controlled hydrophones/accelerometers
   a.2.c. Processing equipment for towed arrays (user-accessible programmability, spectral analysis, digital filtering, beamforming)
   a.2.d. Heading sensors (accuracy <0.5°, designed for >35m or adjustable)
   a.2.e. Bottom/bay-cable hydrophone arrays
   a.2.f. Processing equipment for bottom/bay cable systems
   a.2.g. Accelerometer-based hydro-acoustic sensors (3-axis, sensitivity >48dB, >35m, <20kHz)

#### 6A001.b — Correlation-velocity and Doppler-velocity sonar log equipment
1. Correlation-velocity (>500m distance or speed accuracy <1%)
2. Doppler-velocity (speed accuracy <1%)
Note: does not control equipment limited to depth/distance measurement or fish finding; does not control equipment for surface vessels.

### 6A002 — Optical sensors or equipment and components

#### 6A002.a — Optical detectors

a.1. "Space-qualified" solid-state detectors: (a) 10-300nm peak, <0.1% response at >400nm; (b) 900-1200nm peak, time constant <95ns; (c) 1200-30000nm peak; (d) focal plane arrays >2048 elements, 300-900nm peak.

a.2. Image intensifier tubes:
   (a) 400-1050nm peak, with microchannel plate ≤12μm pitch or charge multiplication element ≤500μm pitch, with multialkali photocathode >350 μA/lm or GaAs/GaInAs or other III/V compound photocathode >10 mA/W
   (b) 1050-1800nm peak, similar MCP/charge multiplication, with III/V compound photocathode >15 mA/W
   (c) Specially designed components: MCPs ≤12μm pitch, charge multiplication elements ≤500μm pitch, III/V compound photocathodes

a.3. Non-"space-qualified" focal plane arrays:
   (a) 900-1050nm peak: time constant <0.5ns or charge multiplication >10 mA/W
   (b) 1050-1200nm peak: time constant <95ns or charge multiplication >10 mA/W
   (c) Non-linear (2D) 1200-30000nm
   (d) Linear (1D) 1200-3000nm with scan/cross-scan ratio <3.8 or signal processing in elements
   (e) Linear (1D) 3000-30000nm
   (f) Non-linear (2D) infrared microbolometer 8000-14000nm
   (g) Non-linear (2D) 400-900nm with charge multiplication >10 mA/W at >760nm, >32 elements

Note 2 to a.3: extensive exclusions for non-space-qualified FPAs including multi-element photoconductive cells, pyroelectric detectors, charge multiplication with response limiting mechanism, thermopile arrays <5130 elements.

#### 6A002.b — "Monospectral/multispectral imaging sensors" for remote sensing
1. IFOV <200μrad; or
2. 400-30000nm with digital output AND ("space-qualified" or airborne with non-silicon detectors and IFOV <2.5mrad)
