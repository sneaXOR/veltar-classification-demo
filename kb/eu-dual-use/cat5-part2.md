# Category 5 Part 2 — "Information Security"

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

Note 2: Category 5, Part 2 does not control products when accompanying their user for personal use.

## Cryptography Note (Note 3 to Category 5, Part 2)

5A002, 5D002.a.1., 5D002.b. and 5D002.c.1. do NOT control items meeting ALL of the following:
a. 1. Generally available to the public (sold from stock at retail, over-counter/mail/electronic/telephone); 2. Cryptographic functionality cannot easily be changed by user; 3. Designed for installation by user without further substantial support; 4. Details accessible to competent authority upon request.
b. Hardware components or 'executable software' of items in paragraph a., where: 1. "Information security" is not primary function; 2. Does not change/add cryptographic functionality; 3. Feature set is fixed, not customer-specified; 4. Details available upon request.

Note to Cryptography Note: Item must be of potential interest to wide range of individuals/businesses, price and main functionality available before purchase without consulting vendor.

## 5A2 — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 5A002 — "Information security" systems, equipment and components

a. Designed or modified to use 'cryptography for data confidentiality' with 'described security algorithm', where cryptographic capability is usable/activated/can be activated other than by secure "cryptographic activation":
   1. Items having "information security" as primary function
   2. Digital communication or networking systems/equipment
   3. Computers and items with information storage/processing as primary function
   4. Items where cryptography supports a non-primary function

Technical Notes for 'cryptography for data confidentiality': employs digital techniques performing any cryptographic function OTHER than: authentication, digital signature, data integrity, non-repudiation, digital rights management, entertainment/mass commercial broadcast/medical record encryption, or key management for these functions.

Technical Notes for 'described security algorithm': (a) "symmetric algorithm" with key >56 bits (excl. parity); (b) "asymmetric algorithm" based on: factorisation >512 bits (RSA), discrete logarithms in finite field >512 bits (Diffie-Hellman), discrete logarithms in other groups >112 bits (elliptic curve); (c) "asymmetric algorithm" based on: lattice problems (NewHope, Frodo, NTRUEncrypt, Kyber, Titanium), supersingular isogenies (SIKE), or decoding random codes (McEliece, Niederreiter) — i.e. post-quantum algorithms.

Note 2 extensive exclusions: smart cards, banking cryptographic equipment, portable/cordless radiotelephones, personal area network devices, mobile telecommunications RAN (≤0.1W, ≤16 users), routers/switches/gateways for OAM, general purpose computers with integral published-standard cryptography, connected civil industry application devices.

b. 'Cryptographic activation tokens'
c. Designed or modified for "quantum cryptography" (QKD)
d. Designed for ultra-wideband with cryptographic channelising/scrambling/network identification codes
e. Designed for "spread spectrum" with cryptographic spreading/hopping codes

### 5A003 — Non-cryptographic "information security"
a. Cable systems for detecting surreptitious intrusion (physical layer)
b. Equipment for reducing compromising emanations

### 5A004 — Systems for defeating/weakening/bypassing "information security"
a. Designed for 'cryptanalytic functions' (including reverse engineering)
b. Items to 'extract raw data' from computing/communications device by circumventing authentication/authorisation

## 5B2 — Test, Inspection and Production Equipment
5B002: Equipment for "development"/"production" of 5A002-5A004/5B002.b., and measuring equipment for evaluating 5A002-5A004 or 5D002 functions

## 5D2 — Software
5D002: For "development"/"production"/"use" of 5A002-5A004; "software" having characteristics of 'cryptographic activation token'; "software" performing/simulating functions of 5A002-5A004
Note: 5D002.c.3.b. does not control "intrusion software".

## 5E2 — Technology
5E002: For "development"/"production"/"use" of 5A002-5A004/5B002/5D002; "technology" with 'cryptographic activation token' characteristics
Note: 5E002.a. does not control "technology" for 5A004.b., 5D002.a.3.b. or 5D002.c.3.b.
