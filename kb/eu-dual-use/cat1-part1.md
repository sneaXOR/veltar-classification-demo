# Category 1 (Part 1) — Special Materials and Related Equipment: Systems, Equipment, Test/Production (1A, 1B)

> Source: Regulation (EU) 2021/821, Annex I

## 1A — Systems, Equipment and Components

### 1A001 — Components from fluorinated compounds
a. Seals, gaskets, sealants or fuel bladders for "aircraft"/aerospace, >50% wt of materials in 1C009.b. or 1C009.c.

### 1A002 — "Composite" structures or laminates
a. Made from: 1. organic "matrix" and "fibrous or filamentary materials" in 1C010.c. or 1C010.d.; or 2. prepregs/preforms in 1C010.e.
b. Made from metal or carbon "matrix" and: 1. carbon fibres with "specific modulus" >10.15x10^6 m AND "specific tensile strength" >17.7x10^4 m; or 2. materials in 1C010.c.
Notes: (1) does not control ≤1 m2, ≤2.5 m, >15 mm for civil aircraft repair; (2) does not control semi-finished items for sporting goods, automotive, machine tool, medical; (4) does not control finished items for specific application; (5) 1A002.b.1. does not control mechanically chopped carbon fibres ≤25.0 mm.

### 1A003 — Non-"fusible" aromatic polyimides in film/sheet/tape/ribbon (>0.254mm thick or coated with carbon/graphite/metals/magnetic)

### 1A004 — Protective and detection equipment (non-military)
a. Full face masks, filter canisters for "biological agents", 'radioactive materials', CW agents, "riot control agents" (CA, CS, CN, CR, DM, MPA)
b. Protective suits/gloves/shoes for biological/radioactive/CW agents
c. Detection systems for biological/radioactive/CW agents
d. Electronic equipment for detecting "explosives" residues using 'trace detection' (<1 ppm vapour or 1 mg solid/liquid)

### 1A005 — Body armour
a. Soft body armour (non-military spec)
b. Hard body armour plates ≤level IIIA (NIJ 0101.06)

### 1A006 — IED disposal equipment (remotely operated vehicles, 'disruptors')

### 1A007 — Equipment/devices for initiating charges electrically
a. Explosive detonator firing sets for 1A007.b. detonators
b. Electrically driven explosive detonators: 1. Exploding bridge (EB); 2. Exploding bridge wire (EBW); 3. Slapper; 4. Exploding foil initiators (EFI)

### 1A008 — Charges, devices and components (shaped charges NEQ >90g, ∅≥75mm; linear shaped cutting charges; detonating cord >64g/m; cutters NEQ >3.5 kg)

### 1A102 — Resaturated pyrolised carbon-carbon for space launch vehicles (9A004) or sounding rockets (9A104)
### 1A202 — Composite structures (tubes, 75-400mm ID, from 1C010/1C210 fibres)
### 1A225 — Platinized catalysts for hydrogen isotope exchange (tritium/heavy water)
### 1A226 — Specialised packings for heavy water separation (phosphor bronze mesh, vacuum distillation)
### 1A227 — High-density radiation shielding windows (>0.09m2 cold area, >3g/cm3, ≥100mm thick)

## 1B — Test, Inspection and Production Equipment

### 1B001 — Equipment for "composite" production/inspection
a. Filament winding machines (≥3 servo positioning axes)
b. 'Tape-laying machines' (≥5 servo axes, for "composite" airframe/'missile' structures)
c. Multidirectional weaving/interlacing machines for "composite" structures
d. Equipment for reinforcement fibre production (polymer conversion, CVD, wet-spinning, alumina fibres)
e. Equipment for prepregs by hot melt
f. NDT for "composites" (X-ray tomography, NC ultrasonic ≥4 axes)
g. 'Tow-placement machines' (≥2 servo axes)

### 1B003 — Tools/dies/moulds for "superplastic forming"/"diffusion bonding" of Ti/Al

### 1B102 — Metal powder production equipment (MTCR)

### 1B116-1B119 — Nozzles, batch mixers, continuous mixers, fluid energy mills for energetic materials
